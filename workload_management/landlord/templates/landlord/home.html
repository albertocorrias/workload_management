Manage common academic administration needs such as teaching workload balancing and programme-level accreditation from your browser. No installation required. Free of charge.

Easily keep track of department-level teaching workload, with unlimited number of lecturers and courses, with customizable teaching assignment types, and across many years.

Ease the accreditation process. Define programme-level graduate attributes, single course-level learning outcomes, and keep track of their performance measures over time, including surveys and exam results.

Accreditation features

Handles mapping between SO and CLO, survey measures for both SLO and CLo and direct preformance measures (e.g., exam questions) over the years. Automatically generates SLO achievement report for a given period.

Workload features

Handles multiple workloads overt the years, with summary for lecturers and courses. Customizable teaching assignment types (standard lectures, studios, supervision, etc) applicable to all workloads in the department for a given period.
